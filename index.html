Chemical equations

Type an equation or pick elements in the table. We will balance it, check that the atoms add up, and tell you about the reaction if it is in our database.

    Write subscripts as ordinary digits (H2O) and coefficients before the formula (2H2). Separate the two sides with “=” or “→”. You can also type only the reactants: “Zn + HCl”. Ions: Ba2+, SO4 2- or SO4^2-. Organic compounds: write bonds “-”, “=”, “≡” without spaces (CH2=CH2) and the reaction sign as an arrow “→”. Polymers: nCH2=CH2 → (-CH2-CH2-)n.

    Tap elements in the table above — you can pick two or more. We will show reactions from the database whose reactants contain all the picked elements.
